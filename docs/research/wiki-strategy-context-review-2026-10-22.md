# Wiki strategy contexts: set effects and specialist gear

Date: 2026-10-22. Developer research only. Production candidate scope remains the existing 100 exact IDs.

## What this review establishes

The compact original [reviewed-contexts.tsv](../../tools/research/wiki-recommended-equipment/reviewed-contexts.tsv) records twelve independently reviewed set/specialist context facts and one crafting context. Each row has exact example IDs, activity, functional condition, present guard, proposed protection boundary, immutable source revisions and uncertainty. Related example IDs can be alternatives or optional items; they are never implicit AND prerequisites. It is not a copied gear ranking, a positive Alch allowlist or an exhaustive item-variant catalogue.

Primary Wiki pages were fetched directly through the public MediaWiki API with `action=query`, `prop=revisions`, `rvprop=ids|content`, `rvslots=main`, `formatversion=2`, explicit page titles and a descriptive research user agent. The revisions linked below were returned on the review date. Canonical example IDs were checked against the repository registry; colour, charged, degraded and ornament variants require separate identity review before runtime use.

A higher equipment score or even dominance of all ordinary equipment stats does not establish replacement of an effect that is absent from those stats. Conversely, a strategy's budget alternative is not automatically worth preserving forever. The correct question is whether a distinct, selected function survives the proposed replacement.

## Existing protection versus a demonstrated bug

Only the four Dragon dagger IDs in these twelve set/specialist contexts belong to the current reviewed 100-ID Alch scope. Their parenthesised poison variants are already covered by `WikiItemLists.isSpecialAttackWeapon`. Specialist Lance and Fang have existing exact weapon-role protection; Fang and Sun Keris also have special-attack protection. Crystal armour and Sun Keris are not alchable; the plugin's collected values do not supply positive Alch values for untradeable equipment.

For other unreviewed gear, the current fallback requires actual ownership, quantity/value thresholds, and full-stat dominance. Ordinary single-copy sets are not moved merely because a higher-tier item exists. Existing set-family membership controls layout, not explicit Alch protection, so it must not be mistaken for a proven functional replacement rule either.

No current live false-positive bank was proved by this context review. Three tempting examples were checked and rejected:

1. **Void gloves versus Barrows gloves.** Ordinary stats really are dominated: Void 8842 has defences 6 / 6 / 6 / 4 / 6 with no offensive bonuses; Barrows 7462 improves those and adds offence. However, Void is untradeable, so `collectAlchValues` omits its value. Feeding positive synthetic values into a builder test would not reproduce the current live pipeline. Sources: [Void gloves 15304461](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Void_knight_gloves?oldid=15304461), [Barrows gloves 15322401](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Barrows_gloves?oldid=15322401).
2. **Bandos versus Obsidian platebody.** Obsidian 21301 has zero Magic and Ranged attack penalties. Its -15 is Magic **defence**. Bandos 11832 has -15 Magic attack and -10 Ranged attack, so it does not fully dominate the Obsidian body. Confusing these fields creates a fictitious bug. Sources: [Obsidian body 15327955](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Obsidian_platebody?oldid=15327955), [Bandos body 15360501](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Bandos_chestplate?oldid=15360501).
3. **Zamorakian hasta versus Guthan's warspear.** Guthan's active spear has Stab / Slash / Crush 75 / 75 / 75; the hasta has 85 / 65 / 65. The hasta's speed advantage does not establish full-stat dominance. Sources: [warspear 15252857](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Guthan%27s_warspear?oldid=15252857), [hasta 15367140](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Zamorakian_hasta?oldid=15367140).

These checks explain why targeted regression fixtures need real item vectors and the actual value-collection path. They do not establish that all possible banks are safe or that a future classifier may ignore set effects.

## Implemented crafting protection

Rune mace 1432 is a material for Bone mace 28792 when combined with Scurrius' spine 28798. The recipe requires 35 Smithing or a payment to Historian Aldo; the plugin does not inspect skills or assume that the player can immediately craft it. Sources: [Bone mace revision 15192239](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Bone_mace?oldid=15192239), [spine revision 15192238](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Scurrius%27_spine?oldid=15192238), [Scurrius strategy revision 15354846](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Scurrius/Strategies?oldid=15354846).

The new bank-only policy vetoes automatic Rune mace Alch routing when a real spine is recorded and no Bone mace entry exists. A spine placeholder alone is insufficient. An existing Bone mace, including its placeholder, reserves the finished product and releases this additional veto; all normal candidate/value/replacement checks still apply. This protects the whole logical stack because a blueprint cannot split it into two destinations. It is a conservative crafting policy, not a claim that every player wants to make the weapon.

The same predicate removes stale automatic session retention for the Rune mace, so depositing the spine after an earlier analysis takes effect. Explicit player tags and valid saved/captured orders still win in both Main and Ironman. This is one additional restriction inside the existing 100-ID scope, not a new disposal candidate or a generic crafting engine.

## Recommended boundary for subsequent work

Retain the current positive candidate scope and existing owner exceptions. Use reviewed context facts as a conservative veto when proposed future replacement evidence discards an unmodelled effect. A partial or absent set in the bank does not prove the account cannot use it: the plugin deliberately does not inspect equipment or inventory, and active variants/placeholders need identity-aware handling.

Do not create broad automatic disposal rules from strategy-table absence, budget ordering, GE prices, higher general stages, the word "BiS", or these twelve rows. Actual replacement edges need their own paired conditions and realistic bank tests. This document and its TSV do not add runtime calls, game actions or a new runtime engine.
