## What the visible summaries suggest

These are qualitative research signals, not full-layout measurements or approved
categories. A displayed tab can establish co-location of the displayed items,
but cannot show where every item occurs or whether another tab repeats them.

- **Combat-style grouping varies.** IDs 222 and 129 describe or show separate
  style-focused tabs. IDs 114, 119, 178, 264 and 268 show gear from several styles
  in one visible tab. Measure combined versus separate style groups across
  complete, deduplicated inputs before choosing a Main default.
- **Rune and travel grouping varies.** Visible summaries for IDs 97 and 264 put
  some runes and teleport tablets together. ID 270 shows runes/ammunition in one
  tab and travel tablets in another. Evaluate quick access and functional
  families rather than assuming one universal travel tab.
- **Supply and tool roles need independent checks.** ID 178 visibly groups
  several finished potion families; ID 222's description distinguishes materials
  and tools. Complete inputs are needed for dose, outfit and family cohesion.

None of these observations establishes what most Main players do. Small item
counts do not establish early/midgame progression: IDs 114 and 268 explicitly
describe maxed accounts. No additional verified earlygame Main was found in the
targeted public-index searches; this does not prove none exists in the plugin.
