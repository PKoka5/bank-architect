# Bank Architect 0.9.0

## Main and custom presets

Choose **Ironman**, **Main** or a saved custom preset from the single chooser at
the top of the sidebar. Editing a bundled preset creates a custom layout;
returning to Ironman or Main restores its original defaults. Saved custom
preferences, item corrections and captured bank orders remain separate.

Main has one rune/teleport tab, all combat styles together, potion families in
**4–3–2–1** dose order, and herbs, seeds and unfinished potions grouped by stage.
Ironman retains its recipe-oriented arrangement. Combat keeps the best-in-slot
layout options.

## Smarter shared Alch rules

Main and Ironman now use one reviewed replacement policy:

- Replaced ordinary armour moves to Alch while best-owned reviewed gear stays.
- A Dragon axe replaces both Rune and Adamant axes; better mining tools replace
  the Rune pickaxe. Placeholders, broken and inactive tools do not prove upgrades.
- Reviewed basic slash weapons, including Rune battleaxes, can move when an
  approved general-purpose upgrade is actually owned.
- Elemental staffs compare matching rune supply; equivalent ordinary Mystic
  colours keep one stable variant. Your chosen Combat variant takes priority.
- Ordinary Dragon halberds count as Alch stock by default. Personal assignments
  and captured physical bank destinations remain authoritative.

**Alch** is a separately movable tag. All bank actions and alching remain manual.

## Blueprint and placement improvements

Cannon parts stay in compact groups in every gear layout, including partial and
mixed ornamented sets. Spiked boots go to **Quest Items**; heraldic Rune helms
(h1–h5) go to **Clues**. Med helms and chainbodies participate in the reviewed
replacement rules.

**Keep current order** lets each blueprint destination accept its live item
order while retaining manual guidance for transfers. Captured bank blueprints
and personal corrections continue to take priority.

The sidebar shows these notes on its first opening after updating. Dismissing
them is saved locally; normal use then resumes.
